Description

Mil Días (A Thousand Days) is Crowned Heads' most complex blend — a full-bodied Ecuadorian Habano wrapper over a three-region Nicaraguan filler that evolves dramatically across its hour-long smoke. Pepper and dark earth in the first third give way to bittersweet chocolate and leather, finishing with a long, spiced cedar retro.
