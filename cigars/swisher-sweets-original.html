Description

Swisher Sweets is the best-selling cigarillo in the United States — a ubiquitous machine-made smoke found at every gas station and convenience store across the country. The homogenized tobacco wrapper is machine-manufactured and sweetened, producing the brand's signature candy-like sweetness. Not a premium cigar experience by any measure, but a cultural staple and the gateway for millions of people to the broader world of cigars.
