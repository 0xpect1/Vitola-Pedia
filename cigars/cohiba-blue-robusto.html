Description

General Cigar's Cohiba Blue is the mild, approachable counterpart to the Black line — using a silky Ecuadorian Connecticut wrapper over Dominican and Nicaraguan long-filler for a smooth, creamy, gentle smoke. The iconic blue band gives it a distinctive look in the humidor. A quality mild-to-medium Dominican blend for everyday smoking, it appeals to those who want the Cohiba presentation without bold strength.
