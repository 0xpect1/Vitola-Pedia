Description

The Neanderthal HN takes the CroMagnon concept and dials it to eleven — a dark Nicaraguan Habano Oscuro wrapper over the same three-region Nicaraguan core that created the CroMagnon legend. HN stands for Habano Oscuro Nicaraguense, and the wrapper's extra fermentation delivers tar, espresso, and an almost primal earthiness. Skip Martin created this for cigar smokers who find full-bodied blends too tame.
