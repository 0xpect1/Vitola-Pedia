Description

One of the most popular small-format Cuban cigars on the market, the Half Corona delivers a surprisingly complex 20-minute smoking experience. Despite its compact size, it showcases classic H. Upmann refinement — creamy, honeyed, and nutty with a smooth cedar finish. Packaged in distinctive cardboard boxes, it has become the gold standard for Cuban quick smokes.
