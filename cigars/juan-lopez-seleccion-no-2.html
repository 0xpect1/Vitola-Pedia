Description

One of Cuba's best-kept secrets, the Juan López Selección No. 2 is a robusto that consistently outperforms its price point. Rich and earthy with a creamy backbone, it shares DNA with more expensive Habanos brands while remaining remarkably affordable. Knowledgeable aficionados hoard these, knowing they punch well above their weight class.
