Description

The Grand Reserve is Gurkha's well-aged premium offering, using carefully selected Connecticut Broadleaf Maduro over a Nicaraguan binder and multi-national filler blend. Dark chocolate and earth open the smoke, building to leather and sweet wood as the cigar progresses. One of Gurkha's most consistent and well-reviewed regular production blends.
