Description

The Esplendidos is Cohiba's signature Churchill — a long, elegant cigar that allows the signature Cohiba creaminess and complexity to develop over 90 minutes. First produced in the early 1980s as diplomatic gifts, the Esplendidos became commercially available and is now one of Cuba's most iconic luxury items.
